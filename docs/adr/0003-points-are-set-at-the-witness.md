# Points are set at the Witness; approval records presentation

Until now a Submission's points were entered at approval: the approval panel took the base points and every bonus, and the member first learned their points when they were awarded. #498 (October 2026) moved the judgement to the Witness. The witness, who is looking at the fish, settles the Species, sets the base points (prefilled from the Point class) and every bonus but first-time, in the one transaction that confirms the Witness. Approval becomes the record that the member brought the fry, plants or coral to a meeting: it computes first-time, awards the total, and involves no judgement beyond the second tap.

## Considered options

- **Keep points at approval, show the member a projection earlier.** Rejected: the projection would be a second copy of the rule, and the committee member at the meeting still has to make the call with the fish long gone.
- **A proposal table, copied onto the Submission at approval.** Rejected: an extra table and a copy step for a value that already has columns. The existing columns hold the points from the Witness on; `approved_on IS NOT NULL` stays the one gate on totals, which every total-reporting query already filters on.
- **Set the points at the Witness, on the Submission's own columns.** Chosen.

## Consequences

- `confirmWitness` takes the Species, the base points and the bonuses, validated per Program by a Zod schema (CARES on BAP only, flowered and sexual reproduction on HAP only, base points 5/10/15/20), and writes them with the Witness in one transaction. It binds or rebinds to the Species the witness settled on and gives the Submission the Species' type, class and Program where they disagree, so a mismatch is no longer a refusal; the `MismatchError` is gone. The Program the bonuses are judged against is the Species' Program, the one the Submission is about to have, not the one the member filed under: the panel offers that Program's bonuses, the route's schema checks them against it, and the transition checks them again against the Species it resolves, so a HAP bonus never lands on a row that is becoming BAP.
- A Species with no Point class takes the chosen base points as its Point class, always. The witness assigns points for an unlisted Species on the spot; the manual's majority vote is not modelled.
- A Species the catalogue lacks is recorded on the witness panel (#522): names prefilled from the member's spelling, type and class from the Submission, the Point class and CARES flag chosen there. The Species is created inside the Witness's transaction, so a refused confirmation leaves no orphan and abandoning the panel creates nothing. The changelog's "Settled at the Witness" entry carries a `new_species` change, from which the approval queue derives "New species recorded by the witness"; nothing is stored on the Submission for it. The panel's "Did they mean one of these?" offers the catalogue's closest Species first, so a misspelt name does not become a duplicate.
- `first_time_species` stays NULL until approval, which computes it.
- A member's save that voids the Witness (ADR-0001) clears the points it set. The next witness starts from the catalogue's prefill.
- Points set at the Witness count toward nothing until approval. The invariants: an unapproved row with points is always witnessed; first-time is NULL on every unapproved row.
- Submissions witnessed under the old flow carry no points and are re-witnessed; there is no legacy approval path. Migration 066 (#524) sends every witnessed, unapproved, pointless row back to awaiting a Witness, clearing what a voided Witness clears and the dead `final_submission_on`, and leaves on each a plain-text changelog note attributed to the original witness: "Witness reset when points moved to the Witness; originally witnessed by X on D". The witness panel's header reads the note (`originalWitnessFromNotes`, src/lifecycle/rewitness.ts) and says so, since the fry were seen and only the Species and points are wanted; the clock counts from the reproduction date, so nothing restarts. A one-off post-deploy script (`src/scripts/email-rewitness.ts`, compiled into the image and run with `node` over `fly ssh console`; it calls `sendRewitnessEmails`) tells each affected member once that nothing is wrong, recording the send per member in `settings` so a second run emails nobody twice. The migration discards those old Witnesses (its rollback restores them to the day, from the notes), which is what makes this decision hard to reverse. After it, no witnessed, unapproved row lacks points.
- This supersedes ADR-0001's 2026-09-28 amendment: once the reset has run, no Submission is unbound past its Witness, because the Witness cannot be confirmed without settling the Species. After the Witness, the Species changes only together with the points, through a correction: `bindSpecies` and `adoptSpeciesClassification` are legal only in pendingWitness (#528).
- The member learns the points when witnessed, while there is still time to dispute them, and sees them as "pending approval" until the meeting (#523): on their own Submission page and their own submissions list, itemised over the one Points rule (`pointsBreakdown`), and nowhere else. Public pages, other members' views of a profile and the standings show awarded points only, and a profile's totals sum awarded rows alone, so a witnessed row's columns never read as awarded.
- The member learns the points by email at the Witness (#526): one letter, replacing the "Screening Approved" and "Witness Confirmed" notices, with the subject `Witnessed: <common name>, <total> points pending approval` and Reply-To the committee's address, since a dispute is a reply for now. Its points table is `pointsBreakdown` over the row as the Witness left it, so the lines add up to the total; below it one line names only the bonuses still possible after approval (`bonusesStillPossible`, src/points.ts: the article while none is counted, and on HAP flowering and sexual reproduction while unset), and a first-time line appears only when the Species had no approved Submission at the Witness, a fact the lifecycle passes with the letter (`WitnessedFacts`) and never adds to the total. The letter's facts are worked out once (`witnessLetter`, src/notifications.ts) for the subject, the template and the demo page alike; the waiting period's end comes from the clock (`waitingPeriodEndsOn`, src/lifecycle/state.ts). Before release, confirm the production `adminsEmail` reaches the committee; if not, add a config key for the Reply-To.
- The member's "brought it to a meeting" step goes (#525). A witnessed Submission is in the approval queue the day its waiting period ends, with no member action; the states either side of that step merge under the name `inApprovalQueue`, the `enterApprovalQueue` and `removeFromQueue` moves and their routes are deleted, and `final_submission_on` becomes a dead column, read and written by nothing and never dropped (the denied_* precedent). Return to Draft is legal from the approval queue. The waiting-period email is a reminder to bring it to a meeting, sent once per Submission from the approval queue; the committee digest stops reporting the approval queue, since its rows wait on the member, not the committee.
- Approval is one button (#527). `approve` takes no form: it records presentation at a meeting, decides first-time by counting the Species' approved Submissions inside its own transaction (the first click wins), and awards what the Witness set. The button posts the total it displayed, and the transition refuses with `StalePointsError`, writing nothing, when the total it would award differs; the route answers with a sheet carrying the current total and a fresh Approve. The approval queue page (`admin/queue.pug`, replacing the table) is phone first: ready rows open to the itemised points with first-time (`previewApproval`, the same sum the button posts), a first tap arms Approve and a second confirms, a committee member's own rows have no button, approvals of the last twelve hours are listed as tonight's, and witnessed rows still in their waiting period are greyed with their ready date. The old approval panel and its form (`approvalPanel.pug`, `src/forms/approval.ts`) are gone; the committee's view of a queued Submission names the preview and links to the queue. The invariants gain their other half: a witnessed, unapproved row always carries points, and an approved row always carries a first-time answer.
- The committee corrects the Species and points from the Witness on (#528). `correctPoints` is legal in the waiting period, the approval queue and Approved, committee-only and never the submitter, bound as the confirm is (the Submission's Species or the one the correction names), and takes what the Witness takes (`PointsCorrection`: the Species or a new one to record, the base points and bonuses) plus first-time on an Approved Submission only, with a required reason. One transaction settles the Species (rebinding and adopting its type, class and Program), writes the points, values an unvalued Species, and leaves one `admin_edit` changelog entry; the Witness stays. Before approval nothing is recomputed and the witness email goes again with "Points corrected: <reason>" (`WitnessedFacts.correctionReason`); after approval the feed entry is updated in place, the standing recomputed (in the Program left too, if the Species moved it) and the approval email sent again, which is new: corrections sent nothing before. The form is the witness panel's Species and Points cards over the current values with a reason and Save correction (`admin/witnessPanel.pug` with `correction` set, as the page `admin/correctPoints.pug`), reached from the Submission page and the approval queue row; choosing another Species there asks the form for itself again with that Species settled and its Point class as the base points, and writes nothing until Save. The old create-Species dialog and the review page's bind controls are gone with the plain bind after the Witness.
- The Edit Approved Submission dialog edits the member's details only (#529): the tank, the foods, the dates and the like, which the member can no longer edit once Approved. Its Species and points sections are gone, and it links to the correction form for those. The write is its own move, `editApprovedDetails`, legal on an Approved Submission only, committee-only and never the submitter: a required reason, one `admin_edit` changelog entry, and nothing else follows, no email and no standings recompute, since a detail counts toward nothing. The move refuses a Species or points field outright, and the dialog's schema (`src/forms/editApprovedDetails.ts`) has none, so a points change cannot slip through the dialog. The loose `correctApprovedSubmission`, which wrote any column through the correction's row, is deleted with the old schema (`src/forms/approvedEdit.ts`).
