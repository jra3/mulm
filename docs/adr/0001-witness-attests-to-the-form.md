# A Witness attests to the form, and any member edit after confirmation voids it

#399 (September 2026) ruled that a confirmed Witness attested to the fry and not to the form, so it survived the member's edits and a Return to Draft. Four days later, while designing the Species catalogue, we reversed it: the Witness now attests that the fry exist *and* that the Submission describes them and the breeding situation correctly, including which Species it is bound to. Any change the member makes after confirmation voids the Witness and the Submission awaits a Witness again.

## Considered options

- **Lock the attested fields after witnessing** and let only the committee change them. Rejected: it makes the member ask the committee to fix their own typo.
- **Void the Witness only when an attested field changes**, with a list of attested fields (everything but images, video, article link and notes). Rejected: the list is a second definition of "what the witness saw" that would drift; a witness who also checked the photo would disagree with it.
- **Void on any edit.** Chosen. One rule, no field list, and the cost is a second look by someone who has already seen the fish.

## Consequences

- Return to Draft no longer keeps a confirmed Witness once the member saves anything.
- Requesting changes on a witnessed Submission sends it back through witnessing after the member resubmits.
- Binding to a Species can move to the witness step because the witness now vouches for it. Approval no longer offers a species step; an unbound Submission cannot be witnessed or approved.
- Applies before approval only. Committee corrections to an Approved Submission are Points corrections with a stated reason; Approved is terminal.
